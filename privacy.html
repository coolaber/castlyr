Castlyr · Opening Coach

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

In short

“We” means the developer of Castlyr, named as the seller on its App Store page. Castlyr has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. The app only sends something over the internet when you ask Smart Coach to read your chess.com games, and purchases are handled by Apple.

Your progress

Lessons, tests, practice, daily review, your streak and your settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app.

Smart Coach and chess.com

Only when you tap “Analyse my games” in the Coach tab, the app sends the chess.com username you entered to chess.com’s public data service (the Published-Data API) and downloads that player’s public games from recent months, one request at a time. The request names the app and its contact address; like any website, chess.com also sees your device’s internet (IP) address. Nothing else about you is sent.

The games are analysed on your device. Only counts from them (for example how often a position came up and how those games ended) are kept on your device, never the games themselves. You can forget them at any time in the Coach tab.

chess.com handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Castlyr is not affiliated with Chess.com.

Premium and payments

Premium is a subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app asks the App Store on your device whether your subscription is active, and nothing about your purchase is sent to us.

What the app doesn’t do

It doesn’t collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, and it doesn’t share or sell any data. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Engine evaluations and opening names are part of the app, so using them sends nothing anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, this policy will be updated first, with a new date at the top, and the update will be described in the app’s release notes.
